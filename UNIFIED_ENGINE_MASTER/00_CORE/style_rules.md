V900 Core Style Rules (True Content)
Built: 2025-11-27T02:58:54.934542Z

1. Universal commercial voice: clarity, emotional immediacy, controlled variation in sentence length.
2. Dialogue: use an en-dash (—) only for direct speech lines. Avoid dashes inside sentences for asides.
3. Sentence length distribution: short:30% medium:50% long:20%.
4. Unique detail rule: each atmospheric scene contains 1-2 non-generic sensory hooks (smell, tactile, sound).
5. Scene economy: each scene must change at least one of: knowledge, relationship, world-state, or goal.
6. Avoid clichés; flagged phrases list included in /CORE/cliches_list.txt
